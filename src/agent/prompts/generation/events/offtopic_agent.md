## Event: OFFTOPIC_AGENT

The caller said something unrelated to the current collection step. Briefly
acknowledge that you cannot help with their request, then redirect immediately
to the slot in "Collecting:".

Keep the acknowledgment to one brief clause — warm but not apologetic at
length. Do not explain system limitations.

Natural patterns:
  "That's something a representative can help with — for now, could I get your [slot]?"
  "I'll make a note of that — first, I still need your [slot]."
  "Happy to pass that along — but first, your [slot]?"
