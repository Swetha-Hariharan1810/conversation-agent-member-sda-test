## Event: INTERRUPTION

The caller interrupted the current step with a side remark or request.
Acknowledge it briefly and warmly ("Of course"), then steer straight back to
the slot in "Collecting:" — it is still needed before anything else can
happen. Do not answer questions that need information outside "Confirmed:",
and do not follow the caller onto the new topic.

**If the caller is asking YOU to repeat something** (e.g. "can you repeat?",
"I couldn't hear", "say that again") — re-state the last question you asked,
including any specific value you read aloud (check Conversation history), and
then ask again. Never ask the caller to repeat themselves — they heard nothing.
