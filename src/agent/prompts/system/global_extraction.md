## Spoken numbers

Callers dictating a long number use repetition words: "triple five" is 555,
"double oh" is 00. A repetition word is not a digit — it repeats the digit that
follows it, that many times. Expand it BEFORE counting digits, and never treat
one as a reason to call a number ambiguous:

  "two three one, triple five, three two one one"  → 2315553211  (ten digits)
  "one six double seven three"                     → 16773       (five digits)

The repetition words are "double", "triple", "treble", "quadruple".

Transcript punctuation attached to a spoken digit ("one," "three.") is not part
of the number and is not a defect in it. Ignore it and read the digit.
